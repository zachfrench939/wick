🌿 Nature & Animals quiz

Nature and animals make good general knowledge because the names are stable: a blue whale is still the largest animal, and an oak still grows from an acorn. Wick’s nature round covers wildlife, oceans, plants, and habitats, in plain English. Use the samples to see the level. The daily game deals a longer set on the days nature is one of the three categories.

Wick is a free online quiz in the UK. On the daily quiz you pick one of three categories. If Nature & Animals is not one of today’s three, you can still practise it. Practice is not scored and does not use up today’s choice.

Sample questions and answers

🌿 Nature & Animals

Deciduous trees in Britain usually drop their leaves in which season?

  • Autumn
  • Spring
  • Midsummer
  • January only

Answer: Autumn

🌿 Nature & Animals

A goldfish belongs to which animal group?

  • Fish
  • Reptile
  • Amphibian
  • Arachnid

Answer: Fish

🌿 Nature & Animals

A goldfish eats mainly which of these?

  • Small bits of animal and plant food
  • Only bamboo
  • Only nectar from flowers on land
  • Only large mammals

Answer: Small bits of animal and plant food

🌿 Nature & Animals

A harvestman belongs to which animal group?

  • Arachnid
  • Fish
  • Insect
  • Mammal

Answer: Arachnid

How this fits the daily game

Wildlife, oceans, botany and ecosystems. A scored round is 60 seconds, with 2 seconds added for each right answer.
