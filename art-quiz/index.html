🎨 Art & Culture quiz

Art and culture questions in Wick cover paintings, books, buildings, and myths that a family can talk about afterwards. The point is the fact, not a trick of wording. Sample questions and answers are below. They are a taste of the bank. The one-minute game, and any later archive page, does not reprint the whole round.

Wick is a free online quiz in the UK. On the daily quiz you pick one of three categories. If Art & Culture is not one of today’s three, you can still practise it. Practice is not scored and does not use up today’s choice.
