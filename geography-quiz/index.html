🌍 Geography quiz

A geography quiz is a fair way to test what has stayed put: capitals, flags, rivers, and the places people mix up. Wick’s geography round is written for a UK player, so official capitals and precise wording are used when a short question would have two honest answers. These sample questions and answers are from the same bank as the one-minute game. They are examples, not a full daily set.

Wick is a free online quiz in the UK. On the daily quiz you pick one of three categories. If Geography is not one of today’s three, you can still practise it. Practice is not scored and does not use up today’s choice.

🌍 Geography

Which of these cities is in Tanzania?

  • Dar es Salaam
  • Monterrey
  • Montreal
  • Mosul

Answer: Dar es Salaam

🌍 Geography

In which country is the city of Barcelona?

  • Spain
  • Cuba
  • Czechia
  • Denmark

Answer: Spain

🌍 Geography

The city of Des Moines is in which US state?

  • Iowa
  • Nevada
  • New Hampshire
  • New Jersey

Answer: Iowa

Which continent is the city of Sharjah in?

  • South America

  • Antarctica

Answer: Asia

How this fits the daily game

Capitals, flags, maps and world landmarks. A scored round is 60 seconds, with 2 seconds added for each right answer. The other rules, the streak, and the rankings are explained in the FAQ and on how to play. Past days, once they have ended, are listed in the archive as a short sample, not the full set.
