🍲 Food & Drink quiz

Food and drink questions travel well if they say which place a dish belongs to, and stay honest when two countries claim it. Wick’s food round covers British classics, world dishes, and the words cooks use. The samples show the question, the four choices, and the answer. A full minute of food questions is what you get when you practise the category.

Wick is a free online quiz in the UK. On the daily quiz you pick one of three categories. If Food & Drink is not one of today’s three, you can still practise it. Practice is not scored and does not use up today’s choice.

  • Soya beans
  • Cocoa beans

How this fits the daily game

World cuisine, ingredients, dishes and culinary facts. A scored round is 60 seconds, with 2 seconds added for each right answer.
