Wick

About Wick and how to play

Wick is a free daily general knowledge quiz for a UK audience. From midnight to midnight in London, everyone sees the same three categories and picks one. You have one minute for that category. The other two lock until tomorrow. Practice can be mixed, or any category, and it is not scored.

A candle burns with the 60-second clock. Each correct answer adds 2 seconds. A wrong answer adds no time, shows the right answer, and locks the buttons for 2 seconds. The round ends when the time runs out, the questions run out, or you end it.

The pages are ordinary files. An account is optional. After a scored game, Wick can send the day, the category, how many you got right, and a random device id so it can show a ranking. Practice is not sent. The privacy notice and the cookies page describe that storage. The FAQ answers the short version.

Scoring

  • First correct answer in a row: 100 points.
  • Second in a row: 150.
  • Third: 200.
  • Fourth and beyond: 250.

A wrong answer scores nothing and the answer streak returns to zero. Days played in a row are kept on this device, with badges at 3, 7, 14, 30, 50, and 100 days. One finished category is enough for the daily streak.

Questions and answers

Category pages such as the geography quiz and the science quiz each show a few questions with answers. The archive does the same for days that have ended. Neither page is the full set. The questions were chosen because the facts stay put. They are checked with care, and a wording may still not suit every reader.

Terms

Wick is for fun. Scores have no cash value and are not a certificate. Please keep it kind. The questions are meant to be family-friendly. This is a personal project on GitHub Pages. This page is not legal advice.
