📚 Language & Words quiz

Language and words suit a general knowledge quiz because etymology and idiom do not change with the headlines. Wick asks about alphabets, British and American vocabulary, and phrases such as “once in a blue moon”. These samples are a short set of quiz questions and answers. The daily language round, on the days it appears, uses a different mix.

Wick is a free online quiz in the UK. On the daily quiz you pick one of three categories. If Language & Words is not one of today’s three, you can still practise it. Practice is not scored and does not use up today’s choice.

Sample questions and answers

📚 Language & Words

Which of these is a vowel in English?

  • T
  • E
  • B
  • M

Answer: E

📚 Language & Words

In British English, the storage space at the back of a car is the what?

  • Hood
  • Boot
  • Trunk
  • Fender

Answer: Boot

📚 Language & Words

A person from Albania is described as what?

  • Albanian
  • Cameroonian
  • Canadian
  • Cape Verdean

Answer: Albanian

📚 Language & Words

A person from South Korea is described as what?

  • South Korean
  • Libyan
  • Liechtensteiner
  • Lithuanian

Answer: South Korean

How this fits the daily game

Etymology, idioms, alphabets and vocabulary. A scored round is 60 seconds, with 2 seconds added for each right answer. The other rules, the streak, and the rankings are explained in the FAQ and on how to play. Past days, once they have ended, are listed in the archive as a short sample, not the full set.
