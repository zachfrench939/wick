🏛️ History quiz

History questions in a pub quiz fall apart when the date is fuzzy. Wick sticks to events, monarchs, and treaties whose facts stay put, from Hastings and Magna Carta through to the twentieth century. The samples below are history quiz questions and answers from the game, with the answer shown so you can check them at the table. A scored daily round uses a fresh set and is not printed here in full.

Wick is a free online quiz in the UK. On the daily quiz you pick one of three categories. If History is not one of today’s three, you can still practise it. Practice is not scored and does not use up today’s choice.

Sample questions and answers

🏛️ History

The charge of the Light Brigade was at which battle?

  • Trafalgar
  • Waterloo
  • Balaclava
  • Rorke's Drift

Answer: Balaclava

🏛️ History

Martin Luther King Jr gave the 'I Have a Dream' speech in which year?

  • 1955
  • 1948
  • 1968
  • 1963

Answer: 1963

🏛️ History

Which king returned in the Restoration of 1660?

  • Henry VIII
  • Charles II
  • Charles I
  • James II

Answer: Charles II

🏛️ History

Trooping the Colour marks the official birthday of whom?

  • The British monarch
  • The Prime Minister
  • The Mayor of London
  • The Archbishop

Answer: The British monarch

How this fits the daily game

Empires, battles, monarchs and historic eras. A scored round is 60 seconds, with 2 seconds added for each right answer. The other rules, the streak, and the rankings are explained in the FAQ and on how to play. Past days, once they have ended, are listed in the archive as a short sample, not the full set.
