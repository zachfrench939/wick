🚀 Space quiz

A space quiz is more than naming the planets in order. Wick also asks about moons, constellations, and the missions people in Britain actually followed, from Apollo to Tim Peake. These space questions and answers are samples from the bank. They are not the full list for any coming day, so a later archive page can still be a surprise.

Wick is a free online quiz in the UK. On the daily quiz you pick one of three categories. If Space is not one of today’s three, you can still practise it. Practice is not scored and does not use up today’s choice.

Sample questions and answers

🚀 Space

A rainbow in the sky is made by light and what?

  • Water droplets
  • A prism in orbit
  • Dust from the Moon
  • The ozone only

Answer: Water droplets

🚀 Space

Which planet is named after the Roman god of war?

  • Mercury
  • Jupiter
  • Venus
  • Mars

Answer: Mars

🚀 Space

Pluto was reclassified in 2006 as which of these?

  • A dwarf planet
  • An asteroid of Mars
  • A moon of Neptune
  • A comet

Answer: A dwarf planet

🚀 Space

The constellation Lyra is traditionally pictured as which of these?

  • the dove
  • the lyre
  • the serpent
  • the shield

Answer: the lyre

How this fits the daily game

Planets, constellations, moons and cosmic missions. A scored round is 60 seconds, with 2 seconds added for each right answer. The other rules, the streak, and the rankings are explained in the FAQ and on how to play. Past days, once they have ended, are listed in the archive as a short sample, not the full set.
