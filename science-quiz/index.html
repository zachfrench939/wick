🧪 Science quiz

Science quiz questions should be answerable without a textbook on the table. Wick uses school-level physics, chemistry, and biology, plus at most one or two non-trivial maths questions in a science round. The examples here show the style: four choices, one answer, and no trick that depends on yesterday’s news. The full minute of questions stays in the game.

Wick is a free online quiz in the UK. On the daily quiz you pick one of three categories. If Science is not one of today’s three, you can still practise it. Practice is not scored and does not use up today’s choice.

Sample questions and answers

🧪 Science

Which element has atomic number 14?

  • Silicon
  • Fluorine
  • Francium
  • Gadolinium

Answer: Silicon

🧪 Science

A herbivore eats what?

  • Plants
  • Only meat
  • Only insects
  • Only fish

Answer: Plants

🧪 Science

Which element has the chemical symbol Es?

  • Einsteinium
  • Rutherfordium
  • Samarium
  • Scandium

Answer: Einsteinium

🧪 Science

Which element has atomic number 64?

  • Gadolinium
  • Cadmium
  • Caesium
  • Calcium

Answer: Gadolinium

How this fits the daily game

Physics, chemistry, biology and a little maths. A scored round is 60 seconds, with 2 seconds added for each right answer. The other rules, the streak, and the rankings are explained in the FAQ and on how to play. Past days, once they have ended, are listed in the archive as a short sample, not the full set.
