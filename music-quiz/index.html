🎵 Music quiz

Music rounds are kinder when they name the piece or the band and not a lyric you would have to sing. Wick’s questions cover classical works, British bands, instruments, and the terms on a score. The answers are printed with these samples so you can settle an argument. The daily set, when music is offered, is longer than this page.

Wick is a free online quiz in the UK. On the daily quiz you pick one of three categories. If Music is not one of today’s three, you can still practise it. Practice is not scored and does not use up today’s choice.

Sample questions and answers

🎵 Music

Jerusalem, the hymn, sets words by which poet?

  • William Shakespeare
  • John Keats
  • Lord Byron
  • William Blake

Answer: William Blake

🎵 Music

Johann Sebastian Bach is a composer of which period?

  • Baroque
  • Romantic
  • Modernist
  • Renaissance only

Answer: Baroque

🎵 Music

Which band recorded Yesterday?

  • The Hollies
  • The Rolling Stones
  • The Kinks
  • The Beatles

Answer: The Beatles

🎵 Music

How many musicians are in a string quartet?

  • 5
  • 4
  • 3
  • 8

Answer: 4

How this fits the daily game

Bands, classical, albums, instruments and hits. A scored round is 60 seconds, with 2 seconds added for each right answer. The other rules, the streak, and the rankings are explained in the FAQ and on how to play. Past days, once they have ended, are listed in the archive as a short sample, not the full set.
