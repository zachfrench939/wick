⚽ Sport quiz

Sport is a pub-quiz staple, and it dates quickly if the question is about last night. Wick uses records, rules, grounds, and tournaments that are already in the book: World Cups, Olympics, cricket, and football clubs. These are sample sport questions and answers. Your scored game is still one category, chosen on the day, and it is not copied out in full here.

Wick is a free online quiz in the UK. On the daily quiz you pick one of three categories. If Sport is not one of today’s three, you can still practise it. Practice is not scored and does not use up today’s choice.

Sample questions and answers

⚽ Sport

Who won the men's football World Cup in 2006?

  • Brazil
  • Italy
  • West Germany
  • Uruguay

Answer: Italy

⚽ Sport

Lewis Hamilton is a world champion in which sport?

  • IndyCar
  • Formula 1
  • Rallying
  • MotoGP

Answer: Formula 1

⚽ Sport

The Open is one of the majors in which sport?

  • Golf
  • Tennis
  • Cricket
  • Rowing

Answer: Golf

⚽ Sport

In tennis, a score of zero is called what?

  • Love
  • Nil
  • Duck
  • Blank

Answer: Love

How this fits the daily game

Football, Olympics, tennis, records and legends. A scored round is 60 seconds, with 2 seconds added for each right answer. The other rules, the streak, and the rankings are explained in the FAQ and on how to play. Past days, once they have ended, are listed in the archive as a short sample, not the full set.
