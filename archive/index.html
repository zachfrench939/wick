Past Wick quizzes

Wick is a daily general knowledge quiz. The same three categories are offered to everyone until midnight in the UK, and a free player picks one. This archive starts on 7 November 2026. A page is added only after that London day has ended. It names the categories and prints a few sample questions and answers. It does not print the rest of the round. Today’s questions, and any day still to come, are not here.

Playing those full rounds later is Wick Pro, which is coming soon. There is no price and no email list. You can play today’s quiz now.
