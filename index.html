One minute. One hundred questions.

Everyone gets the same 100 today. A new Wick starts at midnight, UK time. You get one scored go.

  • Four answers. Tap one, or press 1, 2, 3, or 4.
  • A correct answer is 100 points, adds half a second to the clock, and builds a streak up to 250 points.
  • A miss scores nothing and adds no time. It shows the right answer and locks the buttons for 2 seconds. The clock and the candle keep going.
  • The answer streak goes back to zero after a miss. Days played in a row earn badges.
