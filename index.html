Scoring and the two streaks

A correct answer adds 2 seconds. A miss adds none and pauses the buttons for 2 seconds. The candle keeps burning.

Answer streak is right answers in a row inside this round. It returns to zero when you miss.

Daily streak is UK days in a row with a finished scored category. Practice does not change it. One missed day can be covered. Two missed days reset it.
