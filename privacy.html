Wick

Privacy notice

Wick is a quiz on this website. Anyone can play it, including children. This notice is in plain English. It is not a legal essay.

What Wick does not do

  • It does not use cookies.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, or other trackers.
  • It does not show ads.
  • It does not load scripts, fonts, or images from other websites.
  • It does not ask for your name, email, age, or any other personal data.
  • It does not use a third-party script.

What stays on this device

Wick uses the browser’s local storage for these things only:

  • your best score
  • today’s scored result, so the daily game can be played once
  • how many days in a row you have played, the best run of days, and one covered miss so a single forgotten day does not wipe a long streak
  • the dates you have played and the score for each of those days, so the calendar, the week dots, and your own averages can be shown
  • whether sound is on or off
  • a random device ID, used so today’s scored game is counted once

The best score, today’s result, the daily streak, the dates you played, and the sound setting stay on this device. They are strictly necessary for the game. The random device ID also lives in local storage. It is strictly necessary for score comparison, so one device is counted once a day. It is not your name. Today’s rankings use only the day, the score, and that ID. They show no usernames. Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes all of it.

Score comparison

After a scored daily game, Wick sends three things to Supabase, the company that stores the comparison: the day number, how many answers you got right (a number from 0 to 100), and the random device ID. Practice runs are not sent. Supabase is the processor. The project is in the EU, in the London region. The lawful basis is legitimate interests: showing how a result compares with other people who played that day, without identifying anyone. Supabase deletes those rows automatically after 12 months. Wick does not use a third-party script for this. The page calls Supabase itself.

Children

The questions are written to be family-friendly. Wick does not collect personal data from children or from anyone else.

Cookie policy

Wick does not use cookies. It does not set a cookie for login, advertising, or measurement. The things listed above stay in the browser’s local storage because the game needs them: your best score, today’s result, the daily streak and badges, the sound setting, and the random device ID. You can clear that storage in your browser. A phone buzz on a long answer streak, if you have sound switched on, is not stored.
