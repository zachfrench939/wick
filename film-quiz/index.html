🎬 Film & TV quiz

Film and TV questions work in a pub quiz when the film is old enough to have settled. Wick asks about directors, years, and well-known roles, including British television, rather than last weekend’s opening. The samples below are real questions from the game. Playing the category deals a one-minute practice round, separate from today’s scored choice.

Wick is a free online quiz in the UK. On the daily quiz you pick one of three categories. If Film & TV is not one of today’s three, you can still practise it. Practice is not scored and does not use up today’s choice.
