Wick

Wick questions and answers

Practical answers for the free UK quiz. For the legal notice, read privacy and cookies and storage.

What is Wick?

Wick is a free online quiz from the UK. Each London day, everyone sees the same three categories and picks one. You have 60 seconds. A right answer adds 2 seconds. A wrong answer adds none and pauses the buttons for 2 seconds.

Is Wick a general knowledge quiz?

Yes. The bank covers geography, history, science, nature, space, film and TV, music, sport, food and drink, language, art and culture, and tech. It is written as general knowledge, in the style of pub quiz questions, not as a news quiz.

How do I play the daily quiz?

Open the home page, read the three category cards, and choose one. Wick asks you to confirm, because a free player only gets one scored category that day. The other two stay locked until tomorrow. Practice is separate and is not scored.

How does scoring work?

The first correct answer in a row is 100 points. The second is 150, the third is 200, and the fourth and later are 250. A miss scores nothing and resets the answer streak. The number in the share line is how many answers you got right.

What is the daily streak?

A streak is the number of London days in a row you have finished a scored category. One category is enough to keep it. A single missed day can be covered once, and that cover comes back after a 7-day run. Two missed days reset the streak. The count stays on your device.

Can I practise one category?

Yes. Practice is free and is not scored. You can pick any of the twelve categories, or a mixed set. A practice round does not change today’s result, the streak, or the ranking.

Do I need an account?

No. The quiz works with no account. An optional email account exists for people who want one. It is not required to play, and the questions do not ask for your name.

What are the quiz questions and answers on the category pages?

Each category page shows a few real questions from the Wick bank, with the answer printed underneath. They are samples. They are not the full list for today or for a future day.

Where are past daily quizzes?

The archive lists days that have already ended, starting from 7 November 2026. Each past page names that day’s three categories and shows a short sample of questions and answers. The rest of that day’s set is not published. Playing the full archive is part of Wick Pro, which is not on sale yet.

Does Wick use cookies?

The quiz itself does not need a cookie to play. Optional page counts stay off until you allow them. The privacy notice and the cookies page explain what is stored on the device and what a scored game can send.
